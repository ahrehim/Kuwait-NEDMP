Advancing Environmental Data Governance

Second Regional Workshop: From Foundations to Implementation

📍 Manama, Bahrain 📅 20–22 October 2026 🌐 West Asia Region

Background & Rationale

Environmental decision-making in West Asia depends on reliable, timely, and actionable data to address challenges like water scarcity, climate change, pollution, land degradation, and biodiversity loss. Currently, environmental data across the region is distributed among multiple institutions with varying coordination, standards, and capacities.

Building on the 2025 foundational workshop, this second workshop focuses on practical implementation—aligning national frameworks with the voluntary principles of the Global Environmental Data Strategy (GEDS).

GEDS Pillars

  • Management: Governance & workflows
  • Quality & Provenance: Metadata & validation
  • Interoperability: Shared definitions & APIs
  • Accessibility: Open & secure data flows
  • Capacity-Building: Sustained institutional growth

Target Participants

  • Ministry of Environment Officials
  • National Statistical Offices (NSOs)
  • IT & Digital Transformation Units
  • Environmental Data Managers & Analysts
  • UNEP & Regional Partner Delegations

Key Outcomes

  • Institutional Data-Flow Maps
  • National Coordination Frameworks
  • Dataset Standardization Priorities
  • National Capacity Roadmaps
